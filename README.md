# IKEA Customer Analytics: RFM Segmentation

An end-to-end data analytics learning project using SQL, Python, Jupyter Notebook, and Power BI.

## Project Overview

This project explores customer behavior, customer segmentation, and churn analysis through an IKEA-inspired business scenario.

The goal is to understand customer value, identify customers who may be at risk of leaving, and present useful insights through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Business Questions

- Who are the most valuable customers?
- Which customers may be at risk of churning?
- How does customer value differ across segments?
- How can these insights help improve customer retention?

## Tools Used

- **SQL:** Data querying and analysis
- **Python:** Data analysis and customer segmentation
- **Jupyter Notebook:** Python analysis environment
- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard and data visualization

## Project Workflow

1. Defined the business problem and analytical questions.
2. Worked with the dataset and performed data analysis using SQL.
3. Used Python to explore customer behavior and perform RFM segmentation.
4. Built a Power BI dashboard to present the analysis.
5. Explored how the findings could support business decisions.

## Dashboard

The Power BI report includes three pages:

- **Home:** Project introduction and navigation.
- **Overview:** Summary of customer and business metrics.
- **RFM Analysis:** Customer segmentation and segment performance.

## Key Findings

1. Customer Value

Compare revenue contributions across customer segments.

Identify which segments contribute the largest share of revenue.

Explore how customer value differs between segments.

2. Customer Retention

Examine churn rates across customer segments.

Identify segments that may need additional retention efforts.

Consider both customer activity and customer value when prioritizing retention.

3. Customer and Revenue Trends

Explore differences in customer value across countries.

Analyze revenue trends over time.

Identify patterns that could inform future business decisions
